(01) WHO WE ARE

Dialed In CNC Solutions LLC is a precision CNC repair and service company founded by Kevin Steinbrugge in Dayton, Ohio. With over 10 years of hands-on CNC service experience, Kevin brings multi-platform expertise to machine shops and manufacturers who cannot afford downtime, missed deadlines, or inaccurate parts.

We specialize in CNC electrical and mechanical repair, diagnostics, calibration, alignment and preventive maintenance — exclusively for metal manufacturing machines. We service major platforms including Haas, Fanuc, Mazak, Okuma, Siemens, Mitsubishi, DMG MORI, Heidenhain and more.

We are a lean, owner-led operation. You get Kevin — not a ticket number.

Based in Dayton, we travel up to 150 miles for on-site service — putting us within reach of Cincinnati, Columbus, Indianapolis, Lima, Toledo, Northern Kentucky and the wider Ohio manufacturing corridor.

(02) OUR MISSION

To help metal manufacturing shops in the Dayton region and beyond eliminate CNC downtime, restore machine accuracy and maintain reliable production — through expert electrical repair, mechanical repair, diagnostics, calibration and preventive maintenance.

(03) OUR VISION

To become the most trusted independent CNC service partner for machine shops and manufacturers across Ohio and surrounding markets — known for technical precision, fast response and owner-led reliability.
